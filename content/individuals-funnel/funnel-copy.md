# Funnel copy

**Audience:** individual student pilots and pilots in Saudi Arabia, studying for PPL, CPL, IR or ATPL, bilingual.
**Promise:** a free chapter and twenty questions, then a tier that matches the next exam.
**Stages:** short video → link in bio → free sample → email signup → tier offer → upgrade or subscription.

Replace the sample link only after Platform confirms the route. Do not publish prices here; the offer lives on the pricing page.

## Video hook lines

Each line is the first spoken or on-screen sentence; keep the first three seconds honest and specific.

1. You do not need more PDFs. You need a plan for the next thirty days.
2. The question bank remembers what you forget.
3. One chapter. Twenty questions. No card.

## Comment replies

Keep replies short, factual and free of regulatory claims.

1. "Is this official?"
   We are an independent study platform. GACA (gaca.gov.sa) is the authoritative source, and we cite it. The free chapter shows how we do that.

2. "Why pay if there are free notes?"
   The PDFs are yours to keep; the paid part is the question bank you practise with daily. Start free, upgrade when an exam is close.

3. "Is it available in Arabic?"
   Yes. The same material in English and Arabic, with full right-to-left layout.

## Guardrails

- No invented statistics, no pass-rate promises, no "official" wording.
- Cite GACAR only as a source of the study material; never invent a citation or a part number.
- Every public surface carries the non-affiliation notice in both languages.
- Arabic copy needs native review before publication.
